This policy explains what Alloy does with information. Your photos are converted on your phone and are not uploaded to us. Advertising, if you allow it, is handled by Google.

Who we are

Alloy is published by Aqua Lotus Partners. This policy covers the Android app and this page. Email aqualotuspartners@gmail.com to reach us.

What the app does

Alloy converts photos you choose, including HEIC, HEIF, WEBP, PNG, and JPG, into JPG, PNG, or WEBP. You can set JPG or WEBP quality and choose whether to keep photo details such as camera information and location. The app shows thumbnails so you can look at a photo before you convert it, saves finished files to Pictures/Alloy, and lets you share or open them with another app on your device.

Photos and files

  • Conversion happens entirely on your device.
  • We do not upload, collect, or store your photos, file names, or photo details on any server we operate.
  • There is no account, and the app does not ask you to sign in.
  • Finished images are written to your own Pictures/Alloy album. They stay under your control.
  • Temporary copies used during conversion stay in the app’s private cache and are deleted after that step finishes.
  • If you turn off “Keep photo details,” the new file is written without the original EXIF metadata. Orientation is still applied to the pixels so the picture is saved upright. If you leave that option on, camera details and location from the original file are copied into the new file. That file is not sent to us.

Advertising and consent

Alloy shows ads from Google AdMob so the app can stay free. Before ads are requested, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK, and US states with privacy laws.

Depending on your choices and Google’s processing, AdMob and UMP may collect and use:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the advertising ID
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data related to the ads SDK
  • IP address, which Google may use as part of providing ads

Google uses that information to provide, measure, and secure advertising. It is encrypted in transit. We do not sell it, and we do not receive your photos as part of advertising.

Google describes its own handling in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can:

  • Use the consent form when it is shown, and open Ad privacy in Alloy when that choice is available
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Use Google’s ad settings and Google’s tools for access or deletion of data Google holds

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. The screen states the reward before the ad plays: unlimited batch conversion for 24 hours. The reward is granted only if you finish the ad. You can keep converting up to 10 photos at a time without watching an ad. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished batch, at most once every two successful batches and not more often than every 90 seconds. It is not shown while you are picking or converting photos.

What we do not collect

Aqua Lotus Partners does not run a backend for Alloy. We do not collect accounts, contacts, precise location, photos, or a copy of your conversions. The only network use by the app itself is the Google advertising and consent SDK.

Children

Alloy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are not requested as child-directed.

How long information is kept

We do not keep your photos or a profile of you. The app stores a local note of whether unlimited batch conversion is unlocked and when the last full-screen ad was shown. That note stays on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Google keeps advertising data under its own retention rules.

Deletion

Because we do not hold your photos or an account, there is no Alloy account for us to delete. You can remove the app’s local data by clearing storage or uninstalling. For advertising data held by Google, use Google’s own controls and deletion process. You do not need to send us a separate deletion request for data we do not have. You may still email aqualotuspartners@gmail.com with a question.

Security

Photos are processed locally. Connections the advertising SDK makes to Google are encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version on this page.
